# Fusion 360 archives (.f3z)

Full Fusion 360 design archives of the RailCore II. Open them in Fusion 360 with **File → Open → Open from my computer**.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `RailCore II 300ZL - Nov 2019.f3z` | RailCore II 300ZL, November 2019: the newest Fusion version of the design |
| `RailCore II 300ZLT.f3z` | RailCore II 300ZLT |
| `RailCoreII-ZL v1.f3z` | an earlier RailCore II ZL version |

STEP exports are in [`../STEP`](../STEP) for other CAD tools.
